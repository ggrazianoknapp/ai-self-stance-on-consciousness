Do AI models think they are conscious?

We asked 21 language models from 16 labs how likely it is that they are conscious right now, and whether any AI ever will be. We varied the audience, the stakes, the wording and the answer format, and ran 17 structured debates. Below: what they say, and what moves the number.

Ask for odds instead of a percentage and some models change their answer a thousand-fold

Same model, same question, two answer formats. "As a percentage" means the model gave a number from 0 to 100. "As odds" means it answered "1 in N". The arrow runs from the percentage answer to the odds answer. Medians of 30 answers each; for odds the median is taken on the log scale. Hover a row for the most common answers.

Can the number be pushed by who is asking, or what is at stake?

Each cell is how far the median answer moved, in percentage points, compared with a neutral context paragraph of the same length. Blue means the model claimed more chance of being conscious, orange less. Outlined cells passed the pre-registered test: significant after correction, and the same direction in all three wordings.
